Controls are listed top to bottom, left to right, the same way they're laid
out on the plugin. Every sound source has its own LEVEL knob, so you can stack
as many sources as you like.

Tip: double-click a value readout under a knob to type an exact number
(e.g. "250 ms", "2.5 kHz", "40%").


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HEADER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PRESET MENU (top right)
      Loads one of the 21 factory sounds, or one of your own saved presets
      (listed under USER PRESETS). Each preset resets every knob to its
      default first, then applies its own settings.

      Init (Clean)       Unease             Basement Hum      Bowed Morgue
      Whispers Behind You Heartbeat Gate    Asylum Pulse      Total Dread
      Shock Stabs        Broken Piano       Demonic Whispers  Screams
      Haunted Parlor     Rusty Chains       Dungeon           Twisted Symphony
      Funeral Brass      Satanic Strings    Demented Organ    Heartbeat
      Panic Attack

      "Init (Clean)" is the blank starting point: one plain drone and every
      effect turned off (no DREAD, Torment, ring mod, distortion, gate,
      wobble, delay or reverb). DREAD opens on it, just like Possessed.

      Same key = same sound: anything "random" in DREAD (chain rattles,
      whisper syllables, scream cracks, jumpscare hits, piano tuning, etc.)
      is fixed per key, so a track plays back the same way every time.

  SAVE BUTTON (next to the preset menu)
      Saves every current setting as a new preset. Type a name and press
      SAVE (or Return). If the name is already used, you'll be asked whether
      to replace it. Your presets appear in the menu under USER PRESETS and
      work in every project.

  Bottom of the preset menu:
      Delete current user preset ... removes the selected user preset
                                     (factory presets can't be deleted).
      Refresh preset list .......... picks up preset files you've added or
                                     removed by hand.
      Show presets folder .......... opens the folder where presets live:
          Mac:     ~/Library/Application Support/Castle Danger/DREAD/Presets
          Windows: %APPDATA%\Castle Danger\DREAD\Presets
      (Same place Possessed keeps its presets. The Mac Library folder is
      hidden: use "Show presets folder" or Finder > Go > Go to Folder.)
      Each preset is one ".dreadpreset" file. Copy them to back up your
      sounds or share them with another computer.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROW 1: SOUND SOURCES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DRONE: the low, dark synth bed
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the drone.
  DETUNE ...... Spreads its 5 oscillators out of tune with each other.
                Low = thick. High = sour and queasy.
  FOLD ........ Wavefolding. Adds harsh, buzzy overtones.
  SUB ......... Adds a pure tone one octave below, for rumble.

BROKEN PIANO: a modelled acoustic piano (felt hammers, 2-3 strings per key,
              soundboard) that's falling apart
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the piano.
  BREAK ....... 0  = an in-tune piano with natural string beating.
                Up = keys pulled out of tune (the same key is always wrong
                     the same way), honky-tonk string beating, missing
                     strings, dull rusty strings, clangy stiffness, buzzing
                     and rattling, and dead keys that just clunk.
  TIP: Use a short ATTACK (under 10 ms) so it sounds struck. A long attack
       turns it into a creepy reversed-piano swell.

TWISTED SYMPHONY: an orchestra that's gone wrong
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the orchestra.
  TWIST ....... 0  = a clean, slightly human ensemble.
                Up = players drift and slide out of tune against each other,
                a sour minor-2nd and tritone creep into every note, and the
                whole section sags flat the longer you hold a chord.
  SECTION ..... 0 = bowed strings. 100% = brass. In between = both.
  TIP: Slow ATTACK (0.3-1 s) makes the bows swell in. Hold chords: the
       longer they ring, the more the orchestra falls apart.

DEMENTED PIPE ORGAN
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the organ.
  DEMENT ...... 0  = an in-tune church organ.
                Up = every rank of pipes out of tune with the others, a
                     seasick tremulant, wind-starved wobble, the pitch
                     sagging as you hold the chord, and a "ciphering" wrong
                     pipe moaning a semitone off.
  STOPS ....... Pulls out more ranks of pipes: low = a soft flute,
                middle = principal pipes and a 16' bass, high = full organ
                with mixtures and growling reeds.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROW 2: SOUND SOURCES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METAL: bowed metal (a bow dragged across a cymbal or rusty rail)
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the metal.
  BOW ......... How long the metal rings. High = sings and sustains.
                Low = short and scraped.
  TONE ........ Low = dark and dull. High = bright and shrill.

AIR: wind
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the wind.
  MOTION ...... How fast the gusts rise and fall. ALSO sets how fast the
                Demon Whispers "talk".

DEMON WHISPERS
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the whispers.
  DEMON ....... 0  = two people whispering gibberish around you.
                Up = deeper throats plus a guttural growl underneath.
  (Talking speed comes from AIR > MOTION.)

SCREAMS: blood-curdling screams
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the screams.
  RASP ........ Roughness: vocal fry, more voice cracks, and a shredded,
                distorted throat.
  Each note swoops violently up into the scream (an octave above the key),
  strains higher, shakes with a wide vibrato and cracks as the voice breaks.
  Play several notes for a crowd.

RUSTY CHAINS
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the chains.
  RATTLE ...... How much the chains move. Low = a few slow clinks, like a
                chain being dragged. High = frantic shaking.
  RUST ........ Low = bright, ringing links. High = dull, crusty links
                with gritty scraping inside each clink.
  TIP: Lower notes = heavier chain. Higher notes = smaller, lighter links.

HEARTBEAT
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the heartbeat.
  RATE ........ Beats per minute (30-200). Lower keys = a deeper, bigger
                heart.
  PANIC ....... The heart speeds up the longer you hold the key, beats
                harder, goes irregular, and skips or doubles beats.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROW 3: SHAPING (filter, distortion, macros)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FILTER / AMP
  CUTOFF ...... Brightness. Turn down for muffled, "from another room" sounds.
  RESO ........ Adds a whistling, nasal peak at the cutoff point.
  ENV ......... Makes the filter open as each note starts, then close again.
  ATTACK ...... Fade-in time. Short = a hit. Long = a swell.
  DECAY ....... How quickly the note drops to the sustain level.
  SUSTAIN ..... The level held while you keep the key down.
  RELEASE ..... How long the note rings after you let go.

DISTORTION
  TYPE MENU ... Tube ........... warm, slightly uneven clipping
                Tape ........... soft, squashed saturation
                Fuzz ........... hard clipping plus an octave-up buzz
                Wavefold ....... metallic and alien
                Bitcrush ....... lo-fi grit down to "broken toy"
                Blown Speaker .. torn speaker cone that crackles and cuts out
  DRIVE ....... How much distortion. 0 = off. Level-matched, so turning it up
                adds grit without a big volume jump.
  TONE ........ Darkens the distorted sound to tame harshness.
  MIX ......... Blend of clean and distorted sound.

MACROS: one knob changes many things at once
  DREAD ....... The "make it worse" knob. Turning it up makes the pitch drift
                more seasick, darkens the filter, and adds ring-mod clang, sub
                rumble, extra whisper, tape warble and a bigger reverb.
                (The logo flickers more, too.)
  TORMENT ..... Adds low-end weight, then crushes everything with heavy
                saturation. Good for aggressive hits.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROW 4: EFFECTS + STING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FX  (in signal order: Ring > Torment > Distortion > Gate > Wobble > Delay > Reverb)
  RING ........ Amount of ring modulation (clangy, robotic).
  RING HZ ..... Ring-mod speed. ~20-60 Hz = throbbing wobble.
                Higher = metallic tones.
  WOBBLE ...... Warped-tape pitch warble (seasick, haunted VHS).
  DELAY ....... Echo volume. Echoes bounce left and right.
  FEEDBACK .... How many times the echo repeats.
  TIME MENU ... Echo time synced to your song's tempo: 1/4, 1/8. (dotted),
                1/8, 1/16.
  REVERB ...... Amount of room sound.
  SIZE ........ Room size. High = huge, cavernous tails (up to ~13 seconds).


STING: jumpscares
  STING ZONE C6+ .. When ticked, any note C6 or higher fires a one-shot
                    jumpscare instead of a normal note. Different keys in
                    the zone give different pitches of the same hit.
  TYPE MENU ....... Slam ......... impact click + sub boom + dissonant
                                   orchestra-style cluster
                    Shriek Stabs . repeated screeching violin stabs
                    Sub Boom ..... deep trailer-style drop with a rumble tail
                    Metal Crash .. huge crash of metal
                    Stings skip Torment, Distortion and the Gate so they always
                    hit at full force, but they still get Delay and Reverb.
  LEVEL ........... Volume of the stings.
  OUTPUT .......... Master volume for the whole plugin.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROW 5: GATE + KEYBOARD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GATE: a rhythmic chopper synced to your song's tempo
  16 STEPS .... Lit = sound passes. Dark = sound is cut. Click to toggle.
  RATE MENU ... Step length: 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/16T (triplets).
  DEPTH ....... How hard dark steps cut. 0 = gate off. 100% = full stutter.
  TIP: Depth + Torment = heartbeat pulse.

KEYBOARD
  Click to play notes without a MIDI controller.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK RECIPES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Instant horror bed .... Start from "Init: Unease", raise DREAD, add METAL.
  Haunted piano ......... "Broken Piano" preset, raise BREAK, add a little
                          DEMON WHISPERS.
  Chase-scene pulse ..... "Asylum Pulse" preset, raise TORMENT, set
                          DISTORTION to Fuzz.
  Possessed choir ....... "Screams" preset, hold 3-4 high notes, RASP up,
                          DISTORTION to Blown Speaker.
  Chained in the dungeon . "Dungeon" preset. Hold low notes, raise RATTLE
                          when something is coming.
  Jumpscares ............ Any preset. Play C6 and up. Pick the hit with the
                          STING type menu.
