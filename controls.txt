Controls are listed top to bottom, left to right, the same way they're laid
out on the plugin. Every sound source has its own LEVEL knob, so you can stack
as many sources as you like.

Tip: double-click a value readout under a knob to type an exact number
(e.g. "250 ms", "2.5 kHz", "40%").


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HEADER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PRESET MENU (top right)
      Loads one of the 12 factory sounds. Each preset resets every knob to its
      default first, then applies its own settings.

      Unease (Default)   Basement Hum       Bowed Morgue      Whispers Behind You
      Heartbeat Gate     Asylum Pulse       Total Dread       Shock Stabs
      Broken Piano       Demonic Whispers   Screams           Haunted Parlor
      Rusty Chains       Dungeon

      "Unease (Default)" is the starting-point sound: every knob at its
      default setting. Start here to build your own sound from scratch.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROW 1: SOUND SOURCES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DRONE: the low, dark synth bed
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the drone.
  DETUNE ...... Spreads its 5 oscillators out of tune with each other.
                Low = thick. High = sour and queasy.
  FOLD ........ Wavefolding. Adds harsh, buzzy overtones.
  SUB ......... Adds a pure tone one octave below, for rumble.

METAL: bowed metal (a bow dragged across a cymbal or rusty rail)
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the metal.
  BOW ......... How long the metal rings. High = sings and sustains.
                Low = short and scraped.
  TONE ........ Low = dark and dull. High = bright and shrill.

BROKEN PIANO
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the piano.
  BREAK ....... How wrecked the piano is.
                0   = clean, slightly detuned piano.
                Up  = keys pulled out of tune (the same key is always wrong
                      the same way), rattling strings, missing overtones,
                      and some dead keys that just thud.
  TIP: Use a short ATTACK (under 10 ms) so it sounds struck. A long attack
       turns it into a creepy reversed-piano swell.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROW 2: SOUND SOURCES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AIR: breathy noise shaped like faint vowels
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the air.
  MOTION ...... How fast the vowel shapes drift. ALSO sets how fast the
                Demon Whispers "talk".

DEMON WHISPERS
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the whispers.
  DEMON ....... 0  = two people whispering gibberish around you.
                Up = deeper throats plus a guttural growl underneath.
  (Talking speed comes from AIR > MOTION.)

SCREAMS
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the screams.
  RASP ........ Turns a clean wail into a shredded, gravelly throat.
  Each note swoops up into the scream, and the vibrato gets wilder the longer
  you hold it. Play several notes for a crowd. Best in the middle and upper
  range of the keyboard.

RUSTY CHAINS
  LEVEL ....... Volume of the chains.
  RATTLE ...... How much the chains move. Low = a few slow clinks, like a
                chain being dragged. High = frantic shaking.
  RUST ........ Low = bright, ringing links. High = dull, crusty links
                with grinding friction and creaks.
  TIP: Lower notes = heavier chain. Higher notes = smaller, lighter links.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROW 3: SHAPING (filter, distortion, macros)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FILTER / AMP
  CUTOFF ...... Brightness. Turn down for muffled, "from another room" sounds.
  RESO ........ Adds a whistling, nasal peak at the cutoff point.
  ENV ......... Makes the filter open as each note starts, then close again.
  ATTACK ...... Fade-in time. Short = a hit. Long = a swell.
  DECAY ....... How quickly the note drops to the sustain level.
  SUSTAIN ..... The level held while you keep the key down.
  RELEASE ..... How long the note rings after you let go.

DISTORTION

MACROS: one knob changes many things at once
  DREAD ....... The "make it worse" knob. Turning it up makes the pitch drift
                more seasick, darkens the filter, and adds ring-mod clang, sub
                rumble, extra whisper, tape warble and a bigger reverb.
                (The logo flickers more, too.)
  TORMENT ..... Adds low-end weight, then crushes everything with heavy
                saturation. Good for aggressive hits.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROW 4: EFFECTS + STING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FX  (in signal order: Ring > Torment > Distortion > Gate > Wobble > Delay > Reverb)
  RING ........ Amount of ring modulation (clangy, robotic).
  RING HZ ..... Ring-mod speed. ~20-60 Hz = throbbing wobble.
                Higher = metallic tones.
  WOBBLE ...... Warped-tape pitch warble (seasick, haunted VHS).
  DELAY ....... Echo volume. Echoes bounce left and right.
  FEEDBACK .... How many times the echo repeats.
  TIME MENU ... Echo time synced to your song's tempo: 1/4, 1/8. (dotted),
                1/8, 1/16.
  REVERB ...... Amount of room sound.
  SIZE ........ Room size. High = huge, cavernous tails (up to ~13 seconds).


STING: jumpscares
  STING ZONE C6+ .. When ticked, any note C6 or higher fires a one-shot
                    jumpscare instead of a normal note. Different keys in
                    the zone give different pitches of the same hit.
  TYPE MENU ....... Slam ......... impact click + sub boom + dissonant
                                   orchestra-style cluster
                    Shriek Stabs . repeated screeching violin stabs
                    Sub Boom ..... deep trailer-style drop with a rumble tail
                    Metal Crash .. huge crash of metal
                    Stings skip Torment, Distortion and the Gate so they always
                    hit at full force, but they still get Delay and Reverb.
  LEVEL ........... Volume of the stings.
  OUTPUT .......... Master volume for the whole plugin.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROW 5: GATE + KEYBOARD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GATE: a rhythmic chopper synced to your song's tempo
  16 STEPS .... Lit = sound passes. Dark = sound is cut. Click to toggle.
  RATE MENU ... Step length: 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/16T (triplets).
  DEPTH ....... How hard dark steps cut. 0 = gate off. 100% = full stutter.
  TIP: Depth + Torment = heartbeat pulse.

KEYBOARD
  Click to play notes without a MIDI controller.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK RECIPES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Instant horror bed .... Start from "Init: Unease", raise DREAD, add METAL.
  Haunted piano ......... "Broken Piano" preset, raise BREAK, add a little
                          DEMON WHISPERS.
  Chase-scene pulse ..... "Asylum Pulse" preset, raise TORMENT, set
                          DISTORTION to Fuzz.
  Possessed choir ....... "Screams" preset, hold 3-4 high notes, RASP up,
                          DISTORTION to Blown Speaker.
  Chained in the dungeon . "Dungeon" preset. Hold low notes, raise RATTLE
                          when something is coming.
  Jumpscares ............ Any preset. Play C6 and up. Pick the hit with the
                          STING type menu.
